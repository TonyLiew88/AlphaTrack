VIDEO DEMO: REGISTER BRIGHTCLEAN FROM START TO FINISH
=====================================================

1. Open AlphaTrack > Suppliers & Documents > Add existing supplier.

2. Type these details (step 1 of the form):
     Supplier company name ........ BrightClean Sdn. Bhd.
     Company registration number .. SIM-REG-101
     Supplier contact person ...... Aisyah Rahman
     Supplier contact email ....... yourteam+brightclean@gmail.com
     Internal responsible officer . Farid Hassan (Procurement)
     Procurement approval ref ..... PR-2025-014
     Goods or services provided ... Factory and office cleaning
     Supplier criticality ......... Medium

3. Category (step 2): choose Cleaning Services.

4. Checklist (step 3): keep the 3 suggested documents. Click Save checklist and continue.

5. Upload (step 4): use the PDFs in the folder "Step 1 - upload these 2 while registering":
     - BrightClean_Cleaning_Service_Agreement_2026.pdf
     - BrightClean_Insurance_Certificate_2025-2026.pdf
   Leave "Business Registration Record" empty ON PURPOSE. It shows a missing mandatory document.

6. Review and submit (step 5): click Register supplier.
   The status becomes "Pending documents", because one mandatory document is missing.

7. Show the renewal: open BrightClean, find Insurance Certificate, click Upload renewed version,
   and choose the PDF in the folder "Step 2 - upload this later as the renewal":
     - BrightClean_Insurance_Certificate_2026-2027_RENEWAL.pdf
   Version 2 becomes current. Version 1 stays in Version history as "replaced". Nothing is deleted.

Good to know
  - The import and the local preview always leave BrightClean out, so you can do this live.
  - To record again: open Apps Script, choose M1_resetVideoDemo and click Run.
    It removes BrightClean only (its Drive folder goes to the bin); the other 7 suppliers stay.
    Its number is given back, so BrightClean registers under the same SUP number on the next take.
  - Without the reset, AlphaTrack refuses BrightClean because its registration number is taken.
    Deactivating it does not free the number.
