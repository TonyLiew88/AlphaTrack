# test-pdfs: every fake PDF for AlphaTrack

All 20 PDFs are fictional and stamped **SIMULATED DOCUMENT — FOR HACKATHON TESTING ONLY**. They are generated by `dataset/build_dataset.py`; don't edit them by hand.

## What's in here

```
    Step 2 - upload this later as the renewal/
```

### 1. Suppliers already in AlphaTrack (loaded automatically)

Seven suppliers that are already registered when the app starts. You don't upload these yourself: `M1_importDemoDataset` (in Apps Script) or the local preview loads them for you. One folder per company:

| Folder | PDFs | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Kilat Shine Services | 3 | Contract with an automatic renewal clause; Document with no expiry date |
| Perisai Guard Security | 3 | Expired licence |
| Voltline Electrical Engineering | 4 | Contract with no fixed end date; Incorrect company name; Missing expiry information |
| Mutiara Components | 2 | Missing mandatory document |
| Swift Haul Logistics | 3 | Already-ended contract; Conflicting dates |
| Cahaya Facility Services | 2 | Unreadable PDF |
| Kencana Microparts | 0 | A draft registration with no PDFs yet (the folder only has a note) |

### 2. Video demo - BrightClean (you upload these)

The PDFs **you** upload while recording the video, to show a registration from start to finish. Open `READ ME - video steps.txt` in that folder: it has the exact steps and the details to type.

- `Step 1 - upload these 2 while registering`: the 2 PDFs for step 4 of the registration form.
- `Step 2 - upload this later as the renewal`: the renewed insurance certificate, uploaded after registering to show version history.

## Putting them in Google Drive

Drag the whole `test-pdfs` folder into My Drive and keep its name; the importer finds it by name. This folder is only the *source* of the test files. When a PDF is uploaded *through AlphaTrack*, the app saves its own copy in the `AlphaTrack - Supplier Documents` folder, filed by supplier and document type.
